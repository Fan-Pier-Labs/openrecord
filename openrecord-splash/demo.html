A real product, a fake patient

This is the actual OpenRecord experience running in your browser — the same onboarding, the same agent loop, the same 45 MyChart tools. The only difference is that the tools read a fictional record instead of a live Epic portal.

Both clients share one session. Request a refill on the phone, then switch to Claude Desktop and ask what changed.

    What is not real

    • The patient, the hospital, and every number in the record are invented.
    • No portal is contacted and nothing is stored — reloading starts over.
    • The UI is a faithful re-creation, not the shipped binary.
    • Replies are model-generated where a model is available, and pre-written otherwise. The badge up top says which.

    Nothing here is medical advice, and the real product isn't either — it reads your record and helps you ask better questions.
